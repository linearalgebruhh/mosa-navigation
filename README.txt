MOSA: Visual PointNav in Urban Environments

Qualitative recordings of Go2 navigation in five fixed urban test cities.
25 complete episodes: 16 goal arrivals, 6 collisions, and 3 timeouts.
These are selected demonstration cases and do not estimate overall test SR.

Videos combine BEV and unmodified FPV in the upper row, with Chase below.
All outcomes refer to the recorded RTX 3090 reruns.

The site is a standalone static page. Video files are separate release assets.
No ChatGPT account or runtime service is required by the website source.
