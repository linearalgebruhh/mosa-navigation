Evaluation protocol

Experimental setup

All episodes use the same trained MOSA policy, with fixed start positions, goals, and initial headings.

  • Three synchronized viewsBEV: start, goal, and executed path. FPV: unmodified policy RGB input. Chase: external robot view.
  • Frozen perception and locomotionPretrained MOSA navigation policy with frozen DINOv2 perception and a frozen Go2 locomotion policy.
  • Deterministic navigationDeterministic actions; 900 s time limit; 0.5 m goal radius. Original control frequencies, speed limits, and collision settings.
  • Recording conditionsRTX 3090 reruns of fixed test tasks. All episodes are recorded to termination. Trajectories may differ from the original A100 evaluation.
