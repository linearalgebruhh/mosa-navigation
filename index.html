Method overview

MoSA uses integrated self-motion to organize observations in navigation memory. Motion-derived spatial addresses are combined with content queries and keys to guide memory retrieval and updates. A shared prediction residual couples the spatial and content associations during recurrent updates.

Motion-grounded addresses

Self-motion establishes an episode-relative reference frame, encoded with multiscale spatial features.

Joint spatial–content memory

Spatial and content associations jointly determine where observations are stored and how they are retrieved.

Goal-directed navigation

The policy uses observations, proprioception, and a relative point goal to command the robot without a global map input.

Simulation recording protocol

Recording details

All simulation videos use the same trained MoSA policy with fixed starts, goals, and initial headings.

  • Policy and observationsFrozen DINOv2 perception and Go2 locomotion policy. FPV shows the RGB channel of the policy camera; presentation views do not alter the policy input.
  • Episode conditionsDeterministic actions, a 900 s time limit, and a 0.5 m goal radius, retaining the original control frequencies, speed limits, and collision settings.
  • Outcomes and durationEpisodes are recorded to termination at 10 FPS. Success denotes goal arrival; failures are labeled as collision or timeout in the episode details.
  • Evaluation provenanceThese recordings rerun fixed test tasks on RTX 3090 hardware. Labels reflect the recorded runs; trajectories and outcomes may differ from the original A100 evaluation.
